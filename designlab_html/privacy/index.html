Privacy

We collect as little as we can, use it only to talk with you and run founding access, and delete it when you ask. Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

  • When you email us: your email address and what you write.
  • When you book a call: your name, email and the time you pick. Bookings run through Cal.com.
  • When you pay a deposit: payment is handled by Stripe. We see your name, email and that you paid. We never see or store your card details.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels or advertising cookies on this site. Fonts are hosted here, so pages make no requests to other companies.
  • No selling or sharing your information with anyone beyond the services named above.
  • No adding you to a mailing list you didn't ask for.

Your choices

Email hello@designlab.fyi and we will tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. If you ask us to stop contacting you, we stop right away.
