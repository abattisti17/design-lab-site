Impact Zones

We bring deep expertise in each of these areas.

Data privacy

Snowden, Surveillance Capitalism, Signal. Now more than ever consumers are waking up to surveillance around them and choosing privacy. Stand out by standing up for privacy.

Content moderation

Misinformation, toxicity, extremists. The Facebook Files showed us that ensuring the internet is safe is of the utmost importance, and a big miss for established tech. Shake up the status quo with empirically tested content moderation techniques.

Community power

Icebucket challenge, Kickstarter, Trump. The Internet has enabled new forms of power that put users in the driver’s seat. Unlock the potential of community to catalyze your movement.

Regenerative Food

Waste, disconnectedness, extractive agriculture. The world is waking up to our broken food systems and craves a better way of eating. Nurture new ways of connecting to food, culture, and nutrition with groundbreaking innovations.

Follow our latest experiments at FoodLab.
