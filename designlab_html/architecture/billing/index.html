What it does

  • Phase invoices that send themselves

    Enter the fee by phase and percent complete. Invoices go out on your billing day through QuickBooks or Xero.

  • Late payment follow-up

    Polite reminders at 7, 14 and 30 days past due, in your voice. You see who owes what without opening a spreadsheet.

  • Retainers applied automatically

    Collect a retainer at signing and credit it against the final invoice, so nobody has to remember.

  • Consultant pass-through billing

    Bill structural, MEP and other consultant invoices to the client with your markup, and see when they're paid.

  • Reimbursables on the invoice

    Printing, travel and permit fees get tagged to the project and added to the next invoice.

  • Fee alerts

    A short weekly email: which phases have used more fee than the work they've finished.

  • Change requests priced before you start

    Turn a client's "can we also..." email into a priced additional-services approval they sign before you do the work.
