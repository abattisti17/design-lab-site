What it does

  • License and continuing education tracker

    Every architect's licenses, by state, with renewal dates and the education hours each one still needs.

  • Firm registrations by state

    Certificates of authorization and firm registrations, with renewal reminders before they lapse.

  • Seal register

    A log of which drawings were sealed, by whom, and which set went out. Useful when a question comes up years later.

  • Consultant insurance certificates

    Collect certificates from consultants and get a reminder before one expires.

  • Drawing transmittal log

    Which set went to whom, and when. Searchable, without digging through email.

  • Client decision log

    Clients approve decisions from a link, and each approval is saved with a date. A clear record if there's a dispute later.
