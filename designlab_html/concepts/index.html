Six rough ideas for fee tracking and billing

These are early sketches and none of them is precious. Which one would you fight to keep? Which would you throw out? What's missing?

    Idea A

    Burn alert

    Find out a phase is running hot while you can still do something about it.

    • Reads the timesheets you already keep
    • Emails or texts you at 50%, 80% and 100% of each phase's fee
    • Nothing else to learn

    Idea B

    One-click invoice

    Billing day goes from half a day to ten minutes.

    • Pulls hours and percent complete by phase
    • Drafts a percent-complete invoice in your format
    • You review, adjust and send

    Idea C

    Fee board

    Every project's fee health on one screen.

    • Fee, time spent, billed and collected side by side
    • Spots unbilled work and late payers
    • Built for 1 to 20 people

    Idea D

    Contract to fee plan

    Your signed proposal becomes your project budget automatically.

    • Upload a proposal or AIA contract
    • Phases, fees and billing terms are pulled out for you
    • The project is set up and ready to track

    Idea E

    Billing desk

    A person who knows architecture contracts handles your billing.

    • Not software: a part-time billing specialist
    • Sends invoices, follows up on payments, flags overruns
    • You approve, they do the rest

    Idea F

    Fee kit

    A ready-made spreadsheet and monthly routine you run yourself.

    • Works in Excel or Google Sheets, no new software
    • Set up once per project in a few minutes
    • Nothing connects to anything; you stay in control
