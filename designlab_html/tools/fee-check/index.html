Phase fee check

Enter each phase's fee, the hours logged so far and how far along it really is. You'll see which phases are on track, running hot or over fee. No sign-up, and nothing you type leaves your browser.

How the check works

  • Fee used = hours logged × blended rate ÷ phase fee.
  • Projected at finish = value of hours so far ÷ percent complete. It assumes the rest of the phase goes at the same pace.
  • On track when the projection lands within 5% of the phase fee.
  • Running hot when the projection is more than 5% over the phase fee.
  • Over fee when fee used passes 100%.
  • Whole project counts phases that haven't started at their full fee.

Picking a blended rate

Use the average billing rate across the people working on the project. If you'd rather see cost instead of fee, use average salary per hour times your overhead multiplier. Either works, as long as you use the same one every month.

Why percent complete matters

Hours alone don't tell you much. A phase that has used 60% of its fee is fine if it's 70% done, and a problem if it's 40% done. Being honest about percent complete is what makes this useful.
